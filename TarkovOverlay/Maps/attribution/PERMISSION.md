# Permission to use tarkov.dev site and data

Date: 2026-11-04

Chat: Telegram, **EFT TarkDev**

The user asked, in Russian, whether they could use the tarkov.dev site and its data while building their own program for Escape from Tarkov. EFT TarkDev answered yes, and asked to be sent a link to the program later.

Screenshot: [permission-eft-tarkdev-telegram.png](permission-eft-tarkdev-telegram.png)

This covers the Shebuka rasters from `the-hideout/tarkov-dev-svg-maps` (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) shipped as GPS underlays: Customs.svg and its interior crops, Factory.svg and its four floors, GroundZero.svg and its floors, Shoreline.svg and its five floors, Labs.svg and its floors, and the ground overviews for Woods, Lighthouse, Reserve, Interchange, Streets of Tarkov, and Terminal.
